BIOGRAPHY

I DON'T JUST STUDY CODE. I SHIP SYSTEMS.

I study Computer Science at the Mediterranean Institute of Technology (MedTech, South Mediterranean University / SMU) in Tunis, Tunisia, expected graduation 2030. Completing the Tunisian Baccalauréat Technique built my practical intuition in electrical circuitry, applied physics, and physical hardware limits alongside software engineering.

Since 2026, I serve as Webmaster for the IEEE Computer Society SMU Student Branch Chapter. I architected, developed, and deployed the official chapter website at cs-smu.ieee.tn, managing its GitHub CI/CD pipelines and automated registration workflows for hackathons and bootcamps.

In competitive programming and team competitions, I placed Top 2 worldwide in IEEEXtreme's PreXtreme and compete actively with Team TinkyWinky (tinkywinky.tn) and NOSTOS — The Xenia Protocol. Outside coursework, I spend time saltwater spinning fishing and playing competitive tactical games.
